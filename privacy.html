Privacy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains what LEVEL UP LOGIC LTD ("we") does with personal information on leveluplogic.co.uk. We are the data controller. Contact: info@leveluplogic.co.uk.

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no forms or accounts. It uses a self-hosted, cookie-free analytics tool (Umami) that counts page views and records the referring site, browser, device type and country, with nothing that identifies you. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and to keep the site running.

If you email us

We keep your email address and message so that we can reply and, if we work together, to manage that work. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not sell or share your details. Ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Our apps and other sites

The products linked from this site have their own websites. Where a product handles personal information, its own site says how.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email us at the address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
